# APTC Auto Re-Enrollment Elimination Analysis Dashboard
This dashboard analyzes the potential change in uninsurance and fiscal consequences of the proposed policy to eliminate APTC auto reenrollment 

## Live Dashboard
https://avcole38.github.io/Current-Topics-Final-Dashboard-/

# Topic Overview
This dashboard visualizes the projected impact of eliminating automatic re-enrollment for Advanced Premium Tax Credit (APTC) recipients under the ACA Marketplace, as proposed under Section 112201 of the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (OBBBA). The analysis estimates changes in uninsured rates and uncompensated care costs across all 50 U.S. states and the District of Columbia.
Why This Matters
While Section 112201 of the One Big Beautiful Bill Act projects $5.45 billion in federal subsidy savings, these gains are more than offset by a projected $6.10 billion increase in annual uncompensated care costs. Eliminating automatic re-enrollment introduces an administrative burden that disproportionately harms low-income enrollees and vulnerable populations, ultimately reversing coverage gains achieved under the ACA and destabilizing the broader insurance market.

# Dashboard Features
* Interactive U.S. Map — Click any state to see projected uncompensated care increases, new uninsured counts, coverage loss, and uninsured rate changes
*  National Stacked Bar Chart — Current vs. projected total uncompensated care costs
*  Uninsured Rate Scenario Explorer — Enter any hypothetical national uninsured rate to see how costs shift
*  Interactive Data Table — Searchable and downloadable state-level dataset
